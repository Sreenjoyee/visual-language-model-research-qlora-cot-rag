Figure 5. Evidence retrieval pipeline. A text description of the chest radiograph, generated by the LLaMA-3.2-3B decoder, is encoded by MiniLM-L6-v2 into a 384-dimensional query vector q. FAISS IndexFlatL2 identifies the five nearest passages (L2 distance) within the 24,460-entry knowledge base, which aggregates MIMIC-CXR impression sentences, Indiana University chest X-ray reports, ACR/RSNA clinical guidelines, PubMedQA contexts, and four open-access bibliographic sources. The retrieved passages are forwarded to the dual-branch classification head as contextual evidence. During Stage 2 training, the fifth retrieved passage is substituted with a semantically plausible but adversarial snippet with probability 0.20, improving model robustness to misleading evidence. The retrieval depth k = 5 was selected by ablation over k ∈ {1, 3, 5, 7, 10}.
